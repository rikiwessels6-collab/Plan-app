Common questions

How do I add a card?

Tap the round + button. Search, or pick from the list. Tap the magnifier at the top to find any card, or to jot something straight onto a list.

How do I move a card?

Press and hold the card’s title, then drag it. On a computer, drag the title with the mouse. You can also use Move earlier and Move later in the card’s ••• menu.

How do I get my boards on another device?

On the device that has your boards, open Settings, turn on sync, then choose Add another device. Type the code it shows on the other device.

How do I share a board with my partner or family?

Tap the board’s name at the top, then Share board, then Invite someone. Send them the link. They can see and change that board only; your other boards stay private.

Where are Gmail and Calendar in the app?

They work on the Plainsight website for now and are coming to the app in an update.

I deleted something by mistake.

Tap Undo on the message that appears straight after. You can also keep a backup: Settings, Download backup.

How do I delete my account?

Settings, then Delete account. This removes your account and the boards stored for it on our server. See the privacy policy.
