Plainsight

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Plainsight is a planning board for tasks, notes, photos, money and plans. It is built so that your boards belong to you. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing you put in Plainsight is sold or shared with advertisers.

What stays on your device

Everything you put on a board is saved on your device. If you never turn on sync, your boards never leave it.

Sync and shared boards (optional)

If you turn on sync, or share a board with someone, the contents of your boards, including photos, are stored on our server so that your other devices and the people you share with can see them. The server runs on Cloudflare, with its data held in Western Europe.

  • You do not give us an email address or password. Your account is a random identifier, and each of your devices holds its own secret key.
  • If you add a name, it is shown to the people you share a board with.
  • A shared board can be seen and changed by everyone you invite to it. Your other boards stay private.
  • Like any website, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver requests. We do not use it for anything else.

Gmail and Google Calendar (website only, optional)

If you connect a Google account on the Plainsight website, your browser reads your unread email and calendar events directly from Google and shows them on your board. They are not sent to our server. Plainsight asks Google only for read access to Gmail, access to calendar events (so you can add an event), and a private app folder in your Google Drive. You can disconnect at any time in Settings or in your Google account.

Location and weather

Plainsight uses your location only when you ask it to: to pin a place on a card or to show the weather where you are. A pinned location is saved in that card. To show the weather, the location (or the city you type) is sent to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, which does not receive anything else about you.

Photos and camera

Plainsight opens your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to a card. Only the photo you pick is used.

Deleting your data

  • To delete your account and everything stored for it on our server, open Settings and choose Delete account. Boards you share with others stay with them.
  • To delete what is on a device, delete the app, or clear the website's data in your browser.

Children

Plainsight is a general-audience app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
