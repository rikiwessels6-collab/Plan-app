## Description
Plainsight puts everything you're planning on one visual board, so nothing hides in five different apps.

Add the cards you need from nearly 60:
• Today, Week and Month plans, habits, countdowns and birthdays
• Shopping, meals, recipes, packing and wish lists
• Budget, spending and savings goals
• Notes, sticky notes, photos, places and links
• Projects, goals, decisions, priorities and a journal
• A focus timer, the weather and everything coming up

Made to feel effortless:
• Press, hold and drag to arrange your board
• Tap the magnifier to find anything, or jot a thought straight onto a list
• Colour-coded spaces for Me, Home, Work and Money
• Today clears what you finished overnight and keeps the rest
• Undo for everything, in light or dark

Better together:
• Sync your boards between iPhone, iPad and computer, with no email or password
• Share one board with your partner or family: shopping, meals, jobs and trips stay in step for everyone, and your other boards stay private

Private by design: no ads, no tracking, and your boards stay on your device unless you turn on sync.

## App Privacy answers
- **Do you or your partners collect data?** Yes (only when someone turns on sync or shares a board, or uses the weather card).
- **Data linked to the user**, for App Functionality only, not used for tracking:
  - User Content: Photos or Videos; Other User Content (the boards they sync or share)
  - Identifiers: User ID (the anonymous account identifier)
- **Data not linked to the user**, App Functionality: Location, Precise (sent to the weather service only when the person asks for the weather where they are)
- **Tracking:** No.

## Notes for the reviewer
No sign-in is needed: the app works fully offline on one device.
Sync and sharing are optional (Settings, Turn on sync). To try sharing, open a board's name at the top, Share board, Invite someone.
Accounts can be deleted in Settings, Delete account.
